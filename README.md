Entry for the make.fun bounty [Sum up 26 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126).

A 1024×1024 newspaper front page for Thursday 26 November 2026: 40 stories
across 9 regions — 3 drawn lead panels, 12 numbered cards, the UTC Ribbon
timeline, a world map with numbered pins, a 25-item ALSO list, and a DATE
CHECK footer. Every story carries its source and a status chip; disputed
items are labelled CLAIM, never stated as fact. The page is drawn by
`tools/generate.py`; `main.ts` copies `entry.png` to the output directory.
